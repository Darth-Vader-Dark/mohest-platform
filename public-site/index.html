Our mandate

Policy, quality, and opportunity across the higher education system.

MoHEST accredits institutions, sets the national qualifications framework, and coordinates scholarships and research priorities across all ten states.

  • ✓Setting and enforcing national accreditation standards
  • ✓Coordinating government and partner scholarship programmes
  • ✓Recognising foreign qualifications and certificate equivalence
  • ✓Advancing science, technology and applied research capacity
