About the ministry

Building the institutions that build South Sudan.

Our mandate, our values, and the history behind the ministry.

Established to steward higher education across all ten states of South Sudan.
Who we are

A ministry built for a young nation's future.

MoHEST was established as the national authority responsible for higher education, scientific research, and technological development in the Republic of South Sudan. We work with universities, institutes, and community colleges across the country to raise the quality and reach of post-secondary education.

  • ✓National authority for accreditation and quality assurance
  • ✓Custodian of the national qualifications framework
  • ✓Coordinator of government and international scholarship programmes
  • ✓Champion of applied science and research capacity building

Our history

From directorate to ministry.

Higher education policy in South Sudan was first coordinated through a general directorate before being established as a standalone ministry, reflecting the growing importance of universities, research, and technical training to national development. Today MoHEST oversees a system spanning public universities, national institutes, and a growing network of community colleges in every state.
