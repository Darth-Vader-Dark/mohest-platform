Ministry structure

Nine directorates, one ministry.

Each directorate is responsible for a distinct part of the ministry's mandate, coordinated through a single enterprise portal for staff.

ICT

Information & Communications Technology

Runs the enterprise portal, ministry systems, cybersecurity, and technical infrastructure.

HR

Human Resources

Manages ministry staffing, records, employee lifecycle, and organisational structure.

HES

Higher Education Standards

Accredits institutions and maintains the national qualifications framework.

ST

Science & Technology

Directs national research priorities and technology development programmes.

PL

Planning & Statistics

Coordinates sector-wide planning, data, and monitoring & evaluation.

AF

Administration & Finance

Oversees budget, procurement, and day-to-day ministry operations.

SC

Scholarships & International Cooperation

Coordinates government and partner-funded scholarship programmes.

LG

Legal Affairs

Provides legal guidance and drafts ministry regulations and policy instruments.

PR

Public Relations & Media

Manages public communication, press relations, and the media centre.
