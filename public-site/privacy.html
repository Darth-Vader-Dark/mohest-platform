1. Who we are

The Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Technology (MoHEST) is a ministry of the Government of the Republic of South Sudan. Our principal offices are located in Juba, South Sudan. This privacy policy applies to the MoHEST public website (mohest.gov.ss) and all sub-pages operated by the Ministry.

For data protection purposes, MoHEST is the data controller responsible for the personal information collected through this website.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide to us

  • Contact form submissions — name, email address, telephone number, subject, and message content when you use the contact form.
  • Scholarship and application inquiries — information submitted in connection with foreign scholarship programme enquiries.
  • Staff portal accounts — employee usernames, email addresses, and role information used to operate the internal enterprise portal. This is governed by separate employment data policies.

Information collected automatically

  • Server log data — IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps, collected automatically by web server software.
  • Analytics data — aggregated, anonymised usage statistics collected to help us understand how visitors use the website (see Section 6).

Information we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect financial information, national identity numbers, or sensitive personal data (such as health, religious, or political data) through this public website.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries, requests, and contact form submissions.
  • Provide information about ministry programmes, scholarships, and services.
  • Improve the content, design, and performance of this website.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations applicable to the Government of South Sudan.
  • Detect and prevent unauthorised access or misuse of ministry digital services.

We do not use your personal information for commercial marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making that produces legal effects on individuals.

4. Legal basis for processing

Where data protection law applies, we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests — operating a secure, functional public information website and responding to enquiries from citizens.
  • Consent — where you have freely given specific consent, such as when subscribing to ministry communications (where available).
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by South Sudanese law or government policy.

5. Sharing and disclosure

MoHEST does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Other government ministries and agencies — where a matter falls within their mandate and sharing is necessary to serve your enquiry.
  • Technology service providers — third-party vendors who host, support, or operate components of this website under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements — if required to disclose information by law, court order, or at the direction of a competent South Sudanese authority.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website may use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — strictly necessary for the website to function (e.g., session management for the staff portal login). These cannot be disabled without impairing functionality.
  • Analytics cookies — used to collect anonymised data about how visitors interact with the website (e.g., pages visited, time spent). No personally identifiable information is included.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to access public content on this website.

7. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by government records management regulations. In general:

  • Contact form submissions are retained for 12 months unless an ongoing matter requires longer retention.
  • Server access logs are retained for up to 90 days for security and diagnostics.
  • Staff portal data is retained for the duration of employment plus any applicable statutory period.

8. Security

MoHEST takes reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect the personal information we hold, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission using HTTPS / TLS.
  • Role-based access controls limiting who can access personal data within our systems.
  • Regular security reviews of web infrastructure.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to take care when sharing personal information online.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 13.

10. Children's privacy

This website is intended for general public use, including adults and students of higher education. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 16 without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take prompt action to remove it.

11. Links to other websites

This website contains links to external websites operated by universities, partner agencies, and other government bodies. MoHEST is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or applicable law. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

13. Contact us

For questions, concerns, or requests relating to this privacy policy or the handling of your personal data, please contact:

  • Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Technology
  • ICT & Digital Services Department
  • Juba, Republic of South Sudan
  • Email: ict@mohest.gov.ss
  • Or use the contact form on this website.
