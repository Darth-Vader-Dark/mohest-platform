Verification service

Verify a foreign qualification.

Convert academic qualifications earned outside South Sudan to their official national equivalent.

Process

Verify a foreign qualification.

MoHEST converts foreign academic qualifications to their South Sudanese equivalent so holders can apply to study or work.

Step 1

Submit documents

Original or certified certificates, transcripts, and proof of institutional accreditation in the country of study.

Step 2

Verification

The Higher Education Standards directorate verifies the issuing institution and programme accreditation status.

Step 3

Equivalence assessment

Your qualification is mapped to its South Sudanese equivalent under the national qualifications framework.

Step 4

Certificate issued

An official equivalence certificate is issued, valid for university applications and employment purposes.
