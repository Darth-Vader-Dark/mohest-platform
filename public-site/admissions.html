How admission works.

The national admission process, timeline, and entry requirements for South Sudan's public universities.

National policy

How admission works.

The national admission process runs on a single annual cycle, coordinated by MoHEST across all public universities.

Step 1

Secondary certificate results released

The national examination results are published and become the basis for university eligibility.

Step 2

National application window opens

Students submit a single national application indicating up to three institution and programme preferences.

Step 3

Placement & offers

MoHEST coordinates placement across public universities based on results, capacity, and preference.

Step 4

Registration & enrolment

Admitted students register directly with their assigned institution ahead of the academic year.

Entry requirements

What you need to apply.

Minimum academic requirement

A South Sudan Certificate of Secondary Education, or a recognised equivalent processed through certificate equivalence.

Required documents

Certified certificate copies, national ID or passport, passport photographs, and the completed national application form.

Programme-specific requirements

Some programmes (medicine, engineering) set additional subject and grade requirements published each cycle.

Foreign qualification holders

Applicants with qualifications earned outside South Sudan must first obtain certificate equivalence before applying.
